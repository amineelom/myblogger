# 10 Best Free Website Builders in 2025 (Ranked & Reviewed)

Building a website has never been easier. In 2025, anyone—from small business owners to bloggers and freelancers—can launch a professional-looking website **without writing a single line of code**.  

But with so many options, which platform should you choose? To help you decide, we’ve reviewed and ranked the **10 best free website builders in 2025**, comparing features, pricing upgrades, and ease of use.

---

## **1. Wix – Best Overall Free Website Builder in 2025**

Wix continues to dominate the market in 2025. With **drag-and-drop editing**, hundreds of templates, and advanced customization, it’s the go-to choice for beginners and professionals alike.  

- **Pros:** Easy to use, extensive template library, powerful app marketplace.  
- **Cons:** Free plan includes ads and a Wix-branded domain.  

💡 Perfect if you want full creative control without coding.  

---

## **2. WordPress.com – Best for Blogging**

When it comes to content-driven websites, **WordPress.com** is unmatched. Unlike Wix, it’s built with blogging in mind and has powerful SEO features.  

- **Pros:** Great for blogs, scalable with plugins (on paid plans), strong SEO.  
- **Cons:** Free plan has limited themes and storage.  

💡 If you’re debating **Wix vs WordPress vs Squarespace**, WordPress.com wins for content-heavy sites.  

---

## **3. Squarespace (Free Trial) – Best for Design**

Squarespace is known for **stunning, professional-grade templates**. While it doesn’t offer a permanent free plan, its trial lets you build and test before committing.  

- **Pros:** Sleek designs, built-in marketing tools, excellent mobile optimization.  
- **Cons:** No permanent free plan.  

💡 Best for entrepreneurs who want polished, brand-ready websites.  

---

## **4. Weebly – Best for Small Business Sites**

Owned by Square, **Weebly** integrates eCommerce features like payment processing.  

- **Pros:** Easy store setup, drag-and-drop builder.  
- **Cons:** Free plan charges transaction fees and displays ads.  

💡 Ideal for small shops and service providers.  

---

## **5. Webflow – Best for Designers**

Webflow is the **most advanced free website builder** on this list, offering pixel-perfect customization and CMS functionality.  

- **Pros:** Professional design control, CMS features.  
- **Cons:** Steeper learning curve, limited free hosting.  

💡 Perfect for designers who want more flexibility than Wix or Squarespace.  

---

## **6. SITE123 – Best for Beginners**

As the name suggests, SITE123 is about simplicity. You pick a layout, add content, and your site is ready.  

- **Pros:** Fast setup, beginner-friendly.  
- **Cons:** Limited customization and flexibility.  

💡 Great if you want a simple personal site fast.  

---

## **7. Strikingly – Best for One-Page Websites**

Strikingly focuses on **single-page websites**—great for portfolios, resumes, or event pages.  

- **Pros:** Simple, modern templates.  
- **Cons:** Free plan restricts advanced customization.  

💡 Excellent for quick landing pages.  

---

## **8. Google Sites – Best for Simple Projects**

Google Sites integrates seamlessly with Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Drive).  

- **Pros:** 100% free, collaboration-friendly.  
- **Cons:** Limited customization and design flexibility.  

💡 Best for school projects, team documentation, or internal sites.  

---

## **9. Zyro (by Hostinger) – Best for Speed**

Zyro, now part of Hostinger, emphasizes speed and simplicity.  

- **Pros:** Lightning-fast builder, simple templates.  
- **Cons:** Free version has branding and fewer customization options.  

💡 Great for quick, no-fuss websites.  

---

## **10. Jimdo – Best for Small Personal Websites**

Jimdo has been around for years and offers a solid free plan for personal use.  

- **Pros:** Easy to use, beginner-friendly.  
- **Cons:** Ads and limited storage on free plan.  

💡 Good for simple personal projects or hobby websites.  

---

## **Wix vs WordPress vs Squarespace: Which Should You Choose in 2025?**

Choosing between these three giants depends on your goals:  

- **Wix:** Best for creative control and beginners.  
- **WordPress.com:** Best for blogging and content-driven sites.  
- **Squarespace:** Best for professional, design-focused websites.  

👉 If you’re after a **free forever option**, Wix and WordPress.com are better. Squarespace shines if you’re ready to pay for premium features.

## **FAQs: Best Free Website Builders in 2025**

**Q1. Which is the best free website builder in 2025?**  
Wix is the best overall choice for beginners, while WordPress.com is best for blogs.  

**Q2. Is Squarespace free in 2025?**  
No, Squarespace offers only a free trial, not a permanent free plan.  

**Q3. Can I build an online store with a free website builder?**  
Yes, platforms like Weebly and Wix let you build small eCommerce stores on free plans (with limitations).  

**Q4. Which website builder is best for SEO?**  
WordPress.com has the strongest SEO tools, followed by Wix.  

**Q5. Are free website builders good for professional use?**  
Free plans are fine for personal or small projects, but serious businesses should upgrade to remove ads and use a custom domain.  

**Q6. Can I switch platforms later?**  
Yes, but migrating content can be tricky. Choose a builder that fits your long-term needs.

Your choice depends on your goals—but whichever you pick, these platforms ensure you can build a beautiful website in no time. 🚀
