# Amazon vs eBay Affiliate Programs in 2025: Which One Pays More?

If you’re starting out in **affiliate marketing in 2025**, two of the most popular platforms are:  

- **Amazon Associates (Amazon Affiliate Program)**  
- **eBay Partner Network (EPN)**  

Both allow you to earn commissions by promoting products online, but they differ in **payouts, rules, and conversion rates**.  

This post breaks down **Amazon vs eBay Affiliate Programs** so you can decide which is better for you this year.  

---

## 🔎 Quick Overview: Amazon vs eBay Affiliate

| Feature | **Amazon Associates (2025)** | **eBay Partner Network (2025)** |
|---------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| **Commission Rate** | 1% – 10% (category-based) | Up to 70% of eBay’s commission fee |
| **Cookie Duration** | 24 hours | 24 hours (but higher payouts if bid leads to sale) |
| **Product Range** | 12M+ items (global) | 1.5B+ listings (auctions + buy now) |
| **Approval Difficulty** | Medium (must own website or traffic) | Easier (faster acceptance) |
| **Payout** | $10 minimum, monthly | $10 minimum, monthly |
| **Best For** | Beginners, bloggers, niche sites | Deal bloggers, auction niches, collectors |

---

## 💰 Commission Comparison in 2025  

### Amazon Associates
- Categories like **luxury beauty** and **Amazon devices** pay **up to 10%**.  
- Low-margin items (electronics, video games) still around **1–3%**.  
- Amazon’s **trust factor** → High **conversion rate**, even with lower percentages.  

### eBay Partner Network
- Pays based on a **percentage of eBay’s final seller fee**.  
- Can range from **50%–70%** of what eBay earns.  
- Example: If eBay charges the seller a $10 fee, you may earn **$5–$7**.  
- Strong for **collectibles, refurbished products, and auctions**.  

---

## 📌 Which One Pays More in 2025?  

- **Amazon**: Better for **high-converting traffic** and niche blogs. Even 1–3% commissions add up because of the **volume of sales**.  
- **eBay**: Higher potential payouts **per item**, especially with rare or high-ticket products.  

👉 **Winner:** If you want **consistent earnings** → go with **Amazon**. If you’re in **collectibles or auctions niche** → eBay can outperform.  

---

## ⚖️ Pros & Cons  

### ✅ Amazon Associates  
**Pros**  
- Huge product variety.  
- High trust = high conversions.  
- Easy integration with WordPress plugins.  

**Cons**  
- Strict rules (easy to get banned).  
- Only 24-hour cookie.  
- Lower percentage on electronics.  

### ✅ eBay Partner Network  
**Pros**  
- Higher % potential per sale.  
- Works well for collectors and deal seekers.  
- Easier approval.  

**Cons**  
- Auction model means sales aren’t guaranteed.  
- Interface less beginner-friendly.  
- Brand recognition lower than Amazon’s “Add to Cart” magic.  

---

## 🚀 Best Use Cases  

- **Amazon Affiliate**: Tech blogs, lifestyle blogs, parenting sites, product review websites.  
- **eBay Affiliate**: Niche sites around **cars, collectibles, refurbished tech, sneakers, luxury watches**.  

---

## 🌍 Global Reach in 2025  

- Amazon has **localized affiliate programs** (US, UK, India, EU). You must apply separately for each region.  
- eBay’s program is **more unified**, but traffic conversion depends on whether items ship internationally.  

---

## 📈 Which Is Best for Beginners in 2025?  

- If you want **easy, predictable earnings** → Start with **Amazon Associates**.  
- If your niche is **auctions, collectibles, or rare finds** → eBay Partner Network is stronger.  
- Many affiliates use **both**, depending on product type.
