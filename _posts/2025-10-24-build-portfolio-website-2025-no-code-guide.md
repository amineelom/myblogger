## **Planning Your Portfolio Website**

Before you jump into design mode, plan your site’s **structure and content**.  
Ask yourself:
- What’s my niche or area of expertise?  
- Who’s my target audience?  
- What do I want visitors to do (hire me, contact me, view my work)?  

### Suggested page structure:
1. **Home** – Your introduction and value statement  
2. **Portfolio** – Showcase your best work/projects  
3. **About** – Tell your story  
4. **Services** – List what you offer  
5. **Testimonials** – Build credibility  
6. **Contact** – Easy way for people to reach you

## **Step-by-Step: How to Build Your Portfolio Website**

### Step 1: Choose Your Platform  
Pick a **no-code builder** that fits your needs. Beginners love **Wix or Squarespace**, while designers prefer **Webflow or Framer**.

### Step 2: Select a Template  
Use pre-built templates optimized for creative portfolios. Look for:
- Responsive design  
- Image-focused layouts  
- Easy navigation  

### Step 3: Customize Your Design  
Add your personal branding—colors, fonts, and visuals.  
Keep it consistent with your style and niche.

### Step 4: Add Content and Projects  
Upload your work with descriptions that explain **your role, tools used, and results achieved.**

### Step 5: Preview and Publish  
Before hitting publish, test your site on mobile and desktop to ensure everything looks sharp.

## **Choosing the Perfect Domain Name**

Your domain should be:
- **Short and memorable** (e.g., `janesmith.design`)  
- **On-brand** with your niche  
- Ideally a `.com`, `.design`, or `.portfolio` TLD  

Use providers like **Namecheap** or **Google Domains** for easy setup.

## **Designing a Professional Portfolio Layout**

A clean, minimal design always wins.  
Focus on clarity and **visual storytelling** rather than flashy effects.

### Best design tips:
- Use white space strategically  
- Limit color palettes to 2–3 shades  
- Use high-resolution images  
- Keep navigation simple and intuitive  

In 2025, **dark mode and micro-interactions** are trending design choices that enhance user experience.

## **FAQs About Building a Portfolio Website**

**1. Do I need to know coding to build a portfolio in 2025?**  
No! Platforms like Wix, Framer, and Squarespace make it 100% no-code.

**2. How long does it take to build one?**  
With templates and AI design tools, you can launch in a few hours.

**3. Which builder is best for creatives?**  
Framer and Webflow offer more design flexibility, while Wix and Squarespace are best for beginners.

**4. Can I add my own custom domain?**  
Absolutely. Most builders let you connect your own domain name easily.

**5. How do I make my site look professional?**  
Use clean layouts, consistent colors, and real client testimonials.

**6. What’s the best way to promote my portfolio?**  
Share it on LinkedIn, Instagram, and niche communities like Dribbble or Behance.
